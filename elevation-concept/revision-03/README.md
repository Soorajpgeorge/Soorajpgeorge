# Residence elevation — revision 03

[Download the nine-page drawing package](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Soorajpgeorge/Soorajpgeorge/main/elevation-concept/revision-03/Sooraj-Elevation-Revision-03.pdf)

Horizontal wall and exterior-opening locations were traced from the supplied floor-plan PDFs. The package includes the source-plan entrance comparison, west/north/south elevations, roof section, rear courtyard plan, road privacy proposal and opening register.

- Main entrance retained in the north-facing return at the right end of the verandah.
- Separate kids’ windows, living corner window and bedroom bays retained.
- Central roof falls west; the living void and internal courtyard remain open.
- Rear courtyard follows 3.30 × 2.63 m, measured to the compound wall.
- Privacy proposal uses the approximately 4 m forecourt supplied by the owner.

All heights, roof construction, screening and material choices are proposals. Unscheduled opening widths are approximate, graphic-derived values from NTS plans. The undimensioned rear terrace extent remains provisional. This is a concept review package, not a construction drawing set.

The renders come from one source-traced Blender model. No generated illustration is used to establish dimensional alignment.
